# 👋 Hi, I'm Avi Flombaum

**Engineer • Educator • Entrepreneur • CTO @ Innovent Capital**

I'm a product engineer and creative technologist who believes in building tools that make people's lives better. Based in NYC, I'm passionate about Ruby's elegance, Rails' conventions, and the power of web standards.

## 🚀 What I'm About

I'm a first-generation American autodidact who dropped out of college to pursue my passion for technology. In 2012, I founded [Flatiron School](https://flatironschool.com), where I served as Dean and Chief Product Officer for 8 years, helping thousands learn to code. After Flatiron's acquisition, I joined Revature as CPO, continuing my mission to transform technical education at scale.

Currently, I'm the **CTO of Innovent Capital**, where we invest in early-stage startups with products and customers ready to grow.
